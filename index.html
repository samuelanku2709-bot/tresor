Our Craft

Built to survive real days, not just photoshoots

100% Remy human hair

Sourced in single donor bundles so the cuticle stays aligned — it colours, curls and lasts like your own.

Hand-tied, breathable lace

Every unit is knotted by hand strand by strand for a natural hairline that holds up to Ghana heat and humidity.

Matched to your scalp tone

We tint the lace to your skin before it ships, so there's no guesswork or bleaching once it arrives.
